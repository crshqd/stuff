A Message from the Headteacher

Welcome to Ridgefield Community Schools. We believe that every child has the potential to achieve greatness. Our commitment to high academic standards is reflected in our students, who consistently perform significantly above the national average year after year.

But being "academically smart" is only part of the equation. We cultivate a genuine love for learning, critical thinking, and resilience. Our dedicated, highly qualified teaching staff provides tailored support to ensure that every pupil is challenged appropriately and guided to reach their full potential in a safe environment.

We invite you to explore our vibrant community and see firsthand how we are shaping the leaders, thinkers, and innovators of tomorrow.

Sarah Jenkins

Headteacher, Ridgefield Community Schools
