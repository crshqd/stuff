Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from parents and carers.

The school gates open at 8:30 AM. Registration is at 8:45 AM prompt. The school day finishes at 3:15 PM for Reception and Key Stage 1, and 3:20 PM for Key Stage 2.
Pupils are required to wear a grey skirt, pinafore, or trousers, accompanied by a white polo shirt and a navy blue school jumper or cardigan with the school logo. Black school shoes are mandatory (no trainers).
If your child is unwell, please call the school office on 01632 960123 before 9:00 AM on the first day of absence. You can also email the office with the reason for absence.
In line with UK government guidelines, the Headteacher cannot authorise any holidays during term time unless there are exceptional circumstances. Unauthorised absences may result in a penalty notice.
We use a cashless system for all school meals and trips. Parents will be provided with a secure login to top up their child's account online.
The PE kit consists of a plain white t-shirt, navy or black shorts, and black plimsolls or trainers. During colder months, plain navy jogging bottoms are permitted.
Morning break is 10:30-10:45 AM. Lunch is 12:00-1:00 PM for KS1 and 12:30-1:30 PM for KS2.
Yes, our Breakfast Club opens at 7:30 AM, and our After School Club runs until 6:00 PM every weekday.
Yes, packed lunches are welcome. We are a nut-free school, so please ensure no nut products (including peanut butter and certain cereal bars) are included.
Homework is set weekly via our online learning portal. Pupils also receive a reading record and are encouraged to read daily with a parent or carer.
